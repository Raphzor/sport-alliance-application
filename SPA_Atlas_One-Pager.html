A working note, following our conversation

The AI operating layer
for Sport Alliance.

This is how I would build SPA Atlas: the single, governed control plane through which all internal AI flows. Not a chat window bolted onto the business, but the layer that lets every team use AI with confidence, while compliance, access and audit are handled for them. Below is the shape I would propose for the first nine months.

The default is access with guardrails, never blockades. We trust people to do their work, and build the controls in so they don't have to think about them.

Broad access, governance by design. The fastest route to safe adoption is to make the safe path the easy one.

01

The roadmap, in three phases

Phase 1 · Foundation · weeks 1–6

Get everyone using AI safely, with the compliance baseline in place.

Ships in 4–6 weeks

What we do

  • Company-wide rollout of Claude as the primary assistant.
  • A mandatory, non-skippable onboarding: a short interactive checklist, not a 40-page PDF.
  • It covers what AI can and can't do, rights under the EU AI Act, data handling under GDPR, and our internal policy.

What we stand up

  • The AI Policy that governs every phase after this one.
  • Baseline usage logging and auditability from the first login.
  • Access granted only once onboarding is complete, no exceptions, no friction.

Outcome: every employee has Claude access, understands the rules, and the compliance baseline is live.

Phase 2 · Integration · weeks 5–14

Connect Atlas to the core stack, with data access governed centrally.

4–6 week increments

Initial scope

  • Confluence — knowledge base and documentation.
  • Jira — project and issue tracking.
  • Salesforce — CRM and customer data.
  • Slack — comms and async workflows.

How Atlas governs it

  • AI never touches systems directly; every request routes through Atlas.
  • Atlas applies access control, data masking and audit on the way through.
  • Permissions mirror existing roles, no privilege escalation via AI.
  • First agentic workflows: summarisation, Q&A, triage, cross-system search.

Phase 3 · Intelligence · weeks 12+

Turn Atlas from a gateway into an active AI operations layer.

Continuous, short cycles

What teams gain

  • A structured path to propose, build and ship internal AI projects through Atlas.
  • Multi-agent orchestration: specialist sub-agents for HR, finance, product and support, coordinated centrally.
  • Atlas selects the right model or agent for each task type.

Data and AI, back to back

  • AI access policies managed in sync with data governance — quality, lineage, access.
  • Data-pipeline support: agents that trigger, monitor and act on ETL, anomalies and reporting.
  • Good outputs follow good foundations; the two are one programme, not two.

Outcome: Atlas becomes the operating backbone for AI at Sport Alliance, actively participating in how the business runs.

02

Principles that hold across every phase

Guardrails, not blockades

The default is access with controls, not restriction. Trust people to do their work.

Transparency first

People always know when they're using AI, what data it can see, and why.

Compliance by design

GDPR and the EU AI Act are built into the architecture, not bolted on later.

Data + AI are one programme

Data governance and AI governance are managed together, never in separate lanes.

Short cycles

Every phase ships in 4–6 week increments. No big-bang releases.

Enablement over enforcement

Teams are trained and supported, not policed. Adoption follows trust.
